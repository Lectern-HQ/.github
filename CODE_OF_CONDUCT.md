# Code of conduct

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our promise

Everyone who takes part in Lectern's projects, issues, pull requests and discussions can do so without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or whose first language is not English.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Admit mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Lectern.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, personal or political attacks, sexual language or imagery.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who break them repeatedly or seriously.

Report problems to the maintainers privately at **conduct@lectern.example** (to be replaced by the project's contact address). Every report is reviewed, and the person reporting is protected.
