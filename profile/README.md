# Lectern

Turn the university lectures you can already watch into clean, timestamped transcripts, on your own computer.

- Paste a lecture link. Lectern uses the login you already have in your browser, with your permission, and never asks for your password.
- Transcription runs locally. Recordings and transcripts never leave your computer.
- Long silences such as the break are skipped, and repetition loops are prevented.

## Open source

The part that touches your browser data is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does: [lectern-cookie-reader](https://github.com/Lectern-HQ/lectern-cookie-reader) (Apache-2.0).
